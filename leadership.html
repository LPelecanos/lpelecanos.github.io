Development of a structural-health-monitoring pilot for public buildings and bridges in Cyprus, including permanent sensing, ambient-response identification and FE-model correlation.

Assessment of existing infrastructure

Structural assessment and maintenance questions provide direct routes for translating nonlinear analysis, monitoring and reliability research into practice.

Monitoring-informed modelling

Field data from accelerometers, fibre optics and other sensing systems are used to update and interrogate computational models rather than treated as standalone measurements.

Leadership model

Mechanics → data → decision.

The common leadership philosophy is to build research around difficult infrastructure decisions: understand the mechanics, obtain the right measurements, construct an interpretable computational model, and translate the result into design, assessment or resilience practice.
